# A Better Way to Travel

In many cities, people are looking for better ways to travel. Streets are becoming crowded, and air pollution is a serious problem. For this reason, some cities are encouraging residents to walk, ride bicycles, or use public transportation instead of driving every day.

One example is the city of Greenford. Last year, Greenford built several safe bicycle lanes and repaired old sidewalks. It also introduced a mobile application that shows bus times and suggests walking routes. The city did not close all its roads to cars. Instead, it tried to give people more choices.

The changes have already made a difference. More students now ride bicycles to school, and many workers take the bus two or three days a week. Local shop owners were worried at first, but they later found that people who walk often stop at small stores more frequently than drivers do.

Greenford still has problems to solve. Some bicycle lanes are too narrow, and buses can be crowded during rush hour. Nevertheless, the city plans to continue improving its transportation system. Its goal is not to make driving impossible, but to make clean and convenient travel easier for everyone.
