# The Community Garden

When the old parking lot behind Maple Street School became empty, many people thought it would remain unused. However, a group of parents and students had a different idea. They wanted to turn the space into a community garden.

At first, the project seemed difficult. The ground was covered with stones, and there was no water supply nearby. Every Saturday morning, volunteers came with tools. Some removed the stones, while others built wooden boxes for vegetables. The school science teacher showed the students how to test the soil and choose plants that could grow well in the area.

By the middle of summer, the garden was full of tomatoes, beans, and flowers. More importantly, people who had rarely spoken to one another began working together. Older residents shared gardening experience with children, and families often took extra vegetables home to cook.

The garden has changed more than an empty parking lot. It has become a place where neighbors meet, learn, and help one another. The organizers now hope to add a small reading corner next year.
